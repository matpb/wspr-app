FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Wspr really free to start?

Yes. 50 live transcriptions, 5 file transcriptions and the built-in rewrite styles, with no card and no account. Pro unlocks everything for a single $14.99, paid once.

Does my voice stay on my device?

Always. Transcription runs locally with whisper.cpp on your GPU, and your audio never leaves your computer. Rewrite is optional and, when on, sends only the text to the provider you picked, with your own API key.

Which computers does it run on?

macOS, Windows (x64) and Linux (x64). Every feature is on every platform.

Does it work on Wayland?

Yes on KDE Plasma 6 and GNOME 45+, through the desktop portal. Sway and Hyprland work with XWayland present.

Why pay $14.99 when others charge $10 to $15 a month?

One month of Wispr Flow costs about the same as a lifetime of Wspr. Pay once, own it, no recurring charges, and you get on-device transcription, rewriting and unlimited use.

How is it different from built-in dictation?

Built-in dictation has limited accuracy, no custom vocabulary and no AI rewrite. Wspr uses OpenAI's Whisper models across 99 languages, takes custom vocabulary hints, and can rewrite with OpenAI, Anthropic or Google Gemini.

Does it support push-to-talk?

Yes. Turn it on in Settings → General, then hold your hotkey to record and release to transcribe. Quick taps still toggle. Same on macOS, Windows and Linux.

Can I use it in French, Spanish, Japanese…?

99 languages, detected automatically. Wspr writes in the language you speak.
