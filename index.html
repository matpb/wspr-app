Features

Everything you need to turn voice into text

Wspr lives in your system tray and works with any app. Powered by whisper.cpp with GPU acceleration for fast, accurate, on-device transcription.

Live Transcription

Press a global hotkey from any app. Speak naturally and your words are transcribed in real time, then inserted at your cursor or copied to clipboard.

Global Hotkey

Ctrl+Shift+Space by default, fully customizable. Start and stop recording from anywhere without switching apps. Separate hotkey for transcribe + rewrite.

AI Text Rewriting

Polish transcriptions with AI. Built-in styles for minimal, professional, or concise output. Use OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google Gemini with your own API key.

File Transcription

Transcribe audio or video files. Supports MP3, WAV, M4A, MP4, MOV, MKV and more. Ideal for meetings, lectures, and interviews.

99 Languages

Whisper Models

Choose speed or accuracy

Wspr includes five OpenAI Whisper models. Pick the one that fits your workflow — from lightning-fast to maximum accuracy.

  • Tiny (~75 MB) — Fastest, basic accuracy
  • Base (~140 MB) — Fast, good accuracy
  • Small (~460 MB) — Balanced, recommended
  • Medium (~1.5 GB) — High accuracy
  • Large-v3 (~3 GB) — Best accuracy

FAQ

Common questions about Wspr

Is Wspr really free to start?

Yes. Wspr includes 50 free live transcriptions, 5 file transcriptions, and built-in AI rewrite styles at no cost. No credit card or account required. Upgrade to Pro for $14.99 (one-time) to unlock unlimited everything.

Does my voice data stay on my device?

100%. All speech-to-text transcription is processed locally on your device using whisper.cpp with GPU acceleration. Your audio never leaves your computer. Cloud AI is only used for optional text rewriting — and only if you choose to enable it with your own API key.

What platforms does Wspr support?

Wspr runs on macOS (Apple Silicon), Windows (x64), and Linux (x64). All features are available on every platform.

Why pay $14.99 when competitors charge $10–15 per month?

Because one month of Wispr Flow costs the same as a lifetime of Wspr. Pay once, own it forever — no recurring charges. You get on-device transcription, AI rewriting, and unlimited usage for a single payment.

How is Wspr different from built-in dictation?

Built-in dictation has limited accuracy, no custom vocabulary, and can't rewrite your text with AI. Wspr uses OpenAI's Whisper models for superior accuracy across 99 languages, supports custom vocabulary hints, and offers AI-powered text rewriting with OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google Gemini.
