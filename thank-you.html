Thank you for purchasing Wspr Pro!

Your license key has been sent to your email. Here's how to activate it:

1
Check your email

Look for an email from Polar.sh with your license key.

2
Open Wspr

Click the Wspr icon in your menu bar (macOS) or system tray (Windows/Linux), then open Settings.

3
Enter your license key

Paste your license key in the activation field and click Activate.
